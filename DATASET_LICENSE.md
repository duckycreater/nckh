## Privacy notice

The published dataset does **not** contain personally identifiable images of
participants. All raw scans uploaded from end-user devices remain on the
device unless the user explicitly opted into the dataset release pipeline
(opt-in consent at scan time, revocable from the in-app Privacy panel).
